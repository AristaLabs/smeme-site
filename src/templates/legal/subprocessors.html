Arista Labs uses subprocessors—third-party service providers that process personal data or User Content on our behalf to operate the SMEme platform. This page lists the subprocessors we use today. We update it when vendors change.

Where reasoning runs: The automated reasoning engine evaluates published decision trees on application servers hosted by Render Services, Inc. Hosted SMEme does not call OpenAI or Tavily.

For how we collect and use information, see the Privacy Policy.
