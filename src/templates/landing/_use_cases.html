{# Use cases — Build / Run / Explore as blog-style worked examples #}

Prompts are not a place to store business rules.

LLMs gather and interpret context using your approved tools and data sources. Then SMEme reasons logically over your approved decision procedures — built from your policies, procedures, and fresh AI-assisted research.

Capture your decision procedures with AI-assisted chat — client intake, deal approval, escalation, vendor checks, legal reasoning, medical diagnostics — review and authorize. SMEme formalizes it and provides full logical analysis tools you can call directly from chat or deterministic workflows. Your org’s decision support is fully integrated into your AI chat and agentic workflows. No knowledge engineers, ontologies, or graph databases — and no custom software stack to build and maintain. One MCP connection.

01 Build

Author the procedure in chat

“I want to build a new SMEme decision tree. It needs to determine when a U.S. company has an obligation to withhold taxes during a cross-border deal with a foreign business. I’ve added several files that can be taken as authoritative concerning the rules and context to the local project folder. Do a web search and research current IRS, treaty, tariff, and other relevant authorities and updates.”

Local files Web search Policies Authoring tools

What you get

Readable draft — you review, edit, and Deploy

AI helps draft; Deploy is your authorization gate

The agent drafts with your sources. You read and authorize before anything is live for agents.

02 Run

A real matter, in the chat you already use

“Run my SMEme cross-border tax withholding decision tree on the ACME, Inc. matter concerning their sale of asset X to party D. The matter is already tracked in the CRM as matter #123. Check the cloud drive for the associate’s intake memo, and the email correspondence.”

CRM Cloud drive Email Local files

SMEme report

Concluded — withholding obligation applies

Decision path from your Deployed tree · not an LLM judgment call

Your AI gathers and proposes answers with provenance. SMEme asks what’s next and decides what follows.

03 Explore

Same chat, logical follow-ups

  • “What changes if the services are performed outside the United States?”
  • “What facts would have to change to reach a different conclusion?”
  • “Which facts drove this conclusion?”
  • “Would changing this answer break the path the current answers force?”

What-if, how-to-reach, decisive support, path checks — solver analysis, explained in chat

Every follow-up reuses the same authorized tree. The LLM keeps the conversation going; it does not invent the logic.
