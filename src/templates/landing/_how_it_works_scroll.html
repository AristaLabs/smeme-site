{# Intro #}

/ neuro-symbolic AI at scale

From your expertise to a tool your agent runs

SMEme is the reasoning layer your agent calls when judgment has to be repeatable — not a saved prompt or runbook that re-burns tokens re-explaining your methodology every run. Build and edit your decision tree however you work; your agent calls it on real evidence and gets back a deterministic, attributable answer.

{# Metering + privacy notes #}

Your subscription meters MCP tool calls — deploy once; every run uses your monthly allowance. Start free — no credit card. Connect via MCP when you are ready.

On SMEme-hosted, you control whether to edit, download, or delete your decision trees. Deterministic evaluation runs on SMEme infrastructure, not inside the LLM provider. Hosted SMEme does not call a model to draft decision trees. Your agent authors the graph; SMEme validates and saves it. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
