LLMs are great at interpreting context but you need provably correct reasoning. You need Rational AI.

A single MCP connection provides a rational, System 2, logical reasoning tool with no specialized training, software project, or lock-in.

  • Precision. Answers you can stand behind: the same facts produce the same conclusion every time, with a traceable decision path rather than an AI judgment call.
  • Built-in explainability. Ask which facts drove a conclusion, run what-ifs, or see what would have to change to reach a different outcome.
  • Expertise captured and scaled. Build decision procedures in plain chat from your own policies, documents, and research.
  • No new project. Avoid hiring knowledge engineers, building ontologies, or new software project budgets.
  • Works where you already work. A single connection plugs into the AI tools and automated workflows you already use.
  • No lock-in. You own your decision logic and can download, share, or delete it anytime, or self-host the engine in your own environment.
  • Humans stay in charge. Nothing goes live until an expert reviews and approves it, and the reasoning runs outside the AI model.

Rational AI means trust and control over the answers you receive and the automation they drive.

SMEme doesn't just apply rules or walk a flowchart. It reasons logically over the constraints you define in the decision tree. Ask it questions no chatbot can prove.

  • What follows

    A conclusion follows, is ruled out, or still depends on facts you have not admitted.

    “Can we accept this client?”

  • Which facts decided this

    The smallest set of admitted facts that still forces the conclusion.

    “Which facts made this a decline?”

  • What if a fact changed

    Compare this case with one different fact and see whether the conclusion still follows.

    “Would we still decline if the owner were domestic?”

  • What would have to change

    The edits that would make another conclusion follow, or leave it still possible.

    “What would it take to accept them?”

  • What cannot change the result

    Unanswered questions that would not move a conclusion that already follows.

    “Does the screening report still matter?”

  • What the tree allows

    The conclusions the deployed tree can reach.

    “What outcomes can this tree reach?”

Questions

What is SMEme?

SMEme connects to the chatbot you already use. You authorize a decision tree. SMEme compiles that tree and a constraint solver determines what follows.

What is a decision tree?

The procedure you can read. Questions, branches, and outcomes, in plain language. You see the tree. SMEme compiles the authorized tree into an intermediate representation and reasons over it as boolean constraints.

Do I need to be technical?

You don’t have to know how to explain it. You only have to recognize when a draft is right, wrong, or incomplete. No coding, no special syntax, and no knowledge engineers.

What are constraints and evidence?

Constraints are the rules a decision must satisfy, such as your policies, the law, and your own judgment. After you deploy the tree, SMEme applies those rules as written. Evidence is context that has been used to answer a question in your decision tree, with its source attached. Your data stays in the apps you already use.

Why not just ask the chatbot?

A chatbot goes from context to an answer. SMEme puts evidence in the middle and constraints in charge. The reasoning follows the tree you deployed. The facts stay visible. If the evidence conflicts or is incomplete, the answer stays unresolved.

What happens after I build a tree?

You authorize in the editor by choosing Deploy. That saves the version SMEme will reason over. Then set it to Listed, which means it shows up among the SMEme tools in your chat.

Where does my data go?

Your files stay in the apps you already use. The chatbot turns context from those apps into evidence for a question in the tree. The tree does not keep the matter files.

Can I share a tree?

You can download your decision tree. It stays on your account. Giving another account its own copy is not available yet.

What does it cost?

Hosted Free includes 3 decision trees, 150 MCP calls, and 3 SMEme AI tree builds. Deleting a tree frees a slot. It does not add calls. See pricing for Pro and self hosting.

Can developers build with SMEme?

SMEme Core is source available under the fair code SMEme SUL 1.0. The hosted site is a separate service. Start with the MCP docs and the Core repository.
