Questions

What is SMEme?

SMEme connects to the chatbot you already use. You authorize a decision tree. SMEme compiles that tree and a constraint solver determines what follows.

What is a decision tree?

The procedure you can read. Questions, branches, and outcomes, in plain language. You see the tree. SMEme compiles the authorized tree into an intermediate representation and reasons over it as boolean constraints.

Do I need to be technical?

You don’t have to know how to explain it. You only have to recognize when a draft is right, wrong, or incomplete. No coding, no special syntax, and no knowledge engineers.

What are constraints and evidence?

Constraints are the rules a decision must satisfy, such as your policies, the law, and your own judgment. After you deploy the tree, SMEme applies those rules as written. Evidence is context that has been used to answer a question in your decision tree, with its source attached. Your data stays in the apps you already use.

Why not just ask the chatbot?

A chatbot goes from context to an answer. SMEme puts evidence in the middle and constraints in charge. The reasoning follows the tree you deployed. The facts stay visible. If the evidence conflicts or is incomplete, the answer stays unresolved.

What happens after I build a tree?

You authorize in the editor by choosing Deploy. That saves the version SMEme will reason over. Then set it to Listed, which means it shows up among the SMEme tools in your chat.

Where does my data go?

Your files stay in the apps you already use. The chatbot turns context from those apps into evidence for a question in the tree. The tree does not keep the matter files.

Can I share a tree?

You can download your decision tree. It stays on your account. Giving another account its own copy is not available yet.

What does it cost?

Hosted Free includes 3 decision trees and 150 MCP calls. Deleting a tree frees a slot. It does not add calls. See pricing for Pro.

Can developers build with SMEme?

Yes. SMEme is a hosted MCP service. Start with the builders page and the MCP docs.
