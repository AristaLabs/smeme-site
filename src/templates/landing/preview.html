A reasoned cause is how you keep agency.

A decision is yours when you can say why you made it. Rationality is not a personality trait of a model or a promise that an answer sounds right. It is an act of autonomy: a reasoned cause you can examine, question, and choose to act on. Without that cause, you are accepting an output, not exercising agency.

SMEme puts the reason outside the model. You design and authorize a readable decision tree; your AI gathers the facts; and SMEme determines what follows from the procedure you approved. The model proposes, you authorize, and the solver decides what the admitted facts entail. The result is a decision you can inspect, challenge, and stand behind. This is the idea behind our Sovereignty and AI Principles. The decision tree is yours to download, copy, and delete as you see fit. See our Privacy Policy.
