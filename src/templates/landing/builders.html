Five steps, one loop.

The language model proposes. A rule or a person admits. SMEme evaluates. Missing facts send the loop back for more.

  1. 1 · bootstrapGet readyCheck capabilities, cache the calling guidance, list deployed trees.
  2. 2 · proposeAgent proposes factsFrom documents, tools, and conversation.
  3. 3 · admitFacts are admittedBy rule or by a human reviewer. Nothing is evaluated until it’s admitted.
  4. 4 · evaluateSMEme reasonsAdmitted facts against the approved tree. Same facts, same result.
  5. 5 · reportRoute the resultConclusions, open questions, and trace go downstream.
↺Facts still missing? Report loops back to propose.
Isolated evaluation required When a step needs independent verification, SMEme stops instead of faking it. Your harness gets an explicit stop, never a guessed answer.

See it run in a pipeline

A quarterly royalty run triggers the workflow. The agent proposes facts from the agreement, the W-8BEN-E, and email, a reviewer admits each one with its source, and SMEme evaluates: Withhold at reduced treaty rate, plus the trace. The workflow codes the payment, or routes an Unsure answer to tax counsel.
