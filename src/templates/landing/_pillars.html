Decision-trees, not model weights

01 · Encode

Where knowledge lives

Expertise that only lives in prompts or fine-tuned weights is hard to audit and easy to drift. SMEme encodes it as decision-trees you can inspect, version, and deploy.

02 · Engine

Not a flowchart

A diagram for humans isn’t enough. SMEme decision-trees compile, validate, and evaluate deterministically — same facts in, same conclusion out.
